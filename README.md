## 📌 About the Internship

The internship focused on gaining practical experience in **Social Media Marketing and Digital Content Creation** for IGCSEMark, an automated grading platform designed for IGCSE students.

The internship involved planning and creating social media content, producing Instagram Reels, promoting the IGCSEMark website, supporting marketing campaigns, and contributing to brand strategy and audience engagement.

A major focus was on creating short-form video content that could effectively showcase the platform's features, including past-paper practice, automated marking, score generation, and progress tracking.

The internship provided practical exposure to how an educational technology product can be promoted through creative and engaging social media content.
